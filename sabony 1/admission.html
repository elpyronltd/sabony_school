Admissions Process

Your child's journey to a brighter future starts here.

How to Apply

Step 1: Inquiry & Visit

We encourage prospective families to schedule a school tour and attend an information session to learn more about our programs and facilities.

Step 2: Submit Application

Complete our online application form and submit all required documents by the specified deadline.

Step 3: Assessment & Interview

Applicants will undergo an age-appropriate assessment and a family interview to ensure a good fit.

Step 4: Admission Decision

Admission decisions are communicated to families, and enrollment contracts are issued.

Step 5: Enrollment & Orientation

Upon acceptance, complete the enrollment process and attend our new student orientation.

Required Documents

  • Completed Online Application Form
  • Copy of Birth Certificate (Applicant)
  • Copy of Passport/ID (Parents/GHSardians)
  • Previous School Transcripts/Report Cards (Last 2 years)
  • Recommendation Letters (from previous teachers, if applicable)
  • Immunization Records
  • Recent Passport-sized Photographs (2 copies)
  • Application Fee Payment Confirmation

Admission Timeline (2025-2026 Academic Year)

  • September - December: Open House & Inquiry Period
  • January 15: Application Deadline for Early Decision
  • February: Assessments & Interviews for Early Decision
  • March 1: Admission Decisions Released (Early Decision)
  • March 31: Enrollment Contract Deadline (Early Decision)
  • April 15: Regular Application Deadline
  • May: Assessments & Interviews for Regular Applicants
  • June 1: Admission Decisions Released (Regular)
  • June 30: Enrollment Contract Deadline (Regular)
  • August: New Student Orientation

Tuition & Fees

Bright Future School is committed to providing excellent education at an accessible value. Our tuition and fees cover academic programs, extracurricular activities, and access to school facilities.

Item Amount (GHS)

Annual Tuition (Early Childhood) GHS 80,000
Annual Tuition (Primary School) GHS 100,000
Annual Tuition (Middle School) GHS 120,000
Technology Fee (Annual) GHS 2,500
