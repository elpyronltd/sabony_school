About Sabony School Complex

Our Vision & Mission

At Sabony School Complex, we are dedicated to providing a holistic and enriching educational experience for students from Basic 1 to Basic 9. Our curriculum is designed to foster critical thinking, creativity, and a love for learning.

We believe in nurturing every child's potential, equipping them with the knowledge, skills, and values necessary to thrive in an ever-changing world. Our experienced educators are committed to creating a supportive and engaging learning environment.

Join our community where academic excellence meets character development, preparing students to become responsible and impactful global citizens.

Our Academic Programs

Basic 1 - 3 (Lower Primary)

Focus on foundational literacy, numeracy, and early cognitive development through interactive learning.

Basic 4 - 6 (Upper Primary)

Building on foundations with expanded subjects, problem-solving, and introduction to scientific concepts.

Basic 7 - 9 (Junior High)

Comprehensive curriculum preparing students for higher education, including advanced sciences, arts, and vocational skills.

Creative Arts & Sports

Encouraging artistic expression and physical development through various clubs and activities.

Admissions

We welcome prospective students and families to join the Sabony School Complex family. Our admissions process is designed to be straightforward and supportive.
