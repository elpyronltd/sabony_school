More About Sabony School Complex

Our History

Founded in 2005, Sabony School Complex began with a vision to provide quality education and foster holistic development in children from an early age. Starting with just a handful of students and dedicated teachers, we have grown steadily, building a reputation for academic excellence and a nurturing environment.

Over the years, we have expanded our facilities, introduced innovative teaching methodologies, and continuously updated our curriculum to meet global standards, while staying true to our core values.

Our Values

  • Excellence: Striving for the highest standards in academics and character.
  • Integrity: Upholding honesty, respect, and strong moral principles.
  • Innovation: Embracing new ideas and creative approaches to learning.
  • Community: Fostering a supportive and inclusive environment for all.
  • Responsibility: Encouraging accountability and active citizenship.

Our Facilities

Sabony School Complex boasts modern facilities designed to enhance the learning experience:

  • Spacious and well-ventilated classrooms
  • Fully equipped science and computer labs
  • A comprehensive library with diverse resources
  • Dedicated art and music studios
  • Safe and expansive playgrounds for sports and recreation
  • A health bay with a full-time nurse

Our Faculty

Our team of educators at Sabony School Complex is composed of highly qualified, experienced, and passionate professionals. They are committed to providing personalized attention to each student, inspiring them to reach their full potential.

We invest in continuous professional development for our teachers, ensuring they are equipped with the latest pedagogical techniques and subject matter expertise.
